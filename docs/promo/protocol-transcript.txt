### One SQLite database. Three wire protocols. No per-protocol code.

--- GraphQL (HTTP) ---
$ curl -s localhost:5099/graphql -d '{"query":"{products(limit:3,sort:[price_desc]){data{name price}}}"}'
{"data":{"products":{"data":[{"name":"Comfort Laptop X","price":1917.6},{"name":"Modern Laptop Gold","price":1912.39},{"name":"Modern Laptop Neo","price":1888.56}]}}}
--- PostgreSQL wire protocol (psql, TLS + SCRAM-SHA-256) ---
$ psql -c 'SELECT name, price FROM products ORDER BY price DESC LIMIT 3;'
        name        |  price  
--------------------+---------
 Comfort Laptop X   |  1917.6
 Modern Laptop Gold | 1912.39
 Modern Laptop Neo  | 1888.56
(3 rows)
